Privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026.

Budget Import is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it uses the Google Sheets and Google Drive APIs to read and update the owner's own budget spreadsheet and to make a backup copy of it in the owner's Drive. It does not open any other files.

What it stores

The Google sign-in token is saved only on the owner's own computer. The tool keeps no servers and no databases, and it collects no analytics.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or sent to anyone. Google user data is used only to keep the owner's budget spreadsheet up to date.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
